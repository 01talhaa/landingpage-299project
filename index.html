Bangladesh Labor Act, 2006 - Key Points:

  • Scope: Applies to all establishments with 10+ workers
  • Working Hours: Maximum 48 hours per week
  • Overtime: Compensated at 1.5x normal rate
  • Leave: Minimum 2 days per week + annual leave
  • Child Labor: Minimum age 15 years
  • Penalties: Violation fines up to 50,000 BDT

Brief Summary: The Bangladesh Labor Act, 2006 protects workers' rights, sets maximum working hours at 48/week, mandates overtime compensation, ensures minimum leave, prohibits child labor under 15, and penalizes violations up to 50,000 BDT.
